# Anil Bajpai

Anil Bajpai is the founder and CEO behind ABTalks.

He leads ABTalks, an AI-first learning and talent ecosystem focused
on helping students and early-career professionals build practical
skills, create visible proof of work, and become more discoverable
to employers.

Through ABTalks, he drives initiatives including build-in-public
challenges, AI learning programs, workshops, hackathons, and
career-focused community activities.

## Role at ABTalks

- Founder and CEO
- Leads the ABTalks learning and talent ecosystem
- Drives the community's focus on practical building and proof of work
- Hosts and participates in ABTalks conversations around AI, careers,
  technology, leadership, and the future of work
